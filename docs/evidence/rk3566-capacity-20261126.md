The audit record for the budgets and the camera claim in
`priv/profiles/rk3566-tier1.yml`, and for the engine's context count
(`CONTEXTS` in `native/cairn_rockchip/src/rknn.rs`). Each 300 s cell is one run
of `tools/board-pipeline/rk-cell.sh`; the 120 s cells and the probe table ran
study variants of it that are not in this repository. The cells' logs are kept outside this
repository and this file carries the verdicts and the identities. The
one-context record it replaces as the profile's source is
[`rk3566-capacity-20261118.md`](rk3566-capacity-20261118.md).

## Reading a cell

- **Clean is 552 observations per camera in 300 s**, as in the 2026-11-18
  record; in 120 s it is 221.
- A budget is a camera count times the 1.875 passes/s floor rate the loader
  divides by, never arithmetic on a latency.
- Engine p50 is the harness's `p50_ms`: the detect call, the wait for a free
  context and decode/NMS included, so it rises with the cameras sharing an
  engine. `infer latency`, which times the runtime's calls alone, read 242 of
  264 ms (yolox_s 640, seven cameras; a cell that is not a
  row below) and 85 of 95 ms (tiny 416, sixteen) at
  three contexts: the time is inside the runtime, not in the wait.

## Why three: the NPU runs one job at a time

The probe binary, bare runtime (no pipeline), the CPU governor as found:

| Rung | Solo `rknn_run` ms | NPU ms a pass | passes/s ×2 | ×3 | ×4 | ×6 | ×8 | 1 / NPU ms | NPU busy ×4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| tiny 416 | 39 | ≈ 22 | 30.0 | 33.8 | 38.8 | 40.9 | 41.7 | ≈ 46 | 90 % |
| yolox_s 640 | 96 | ≈ 65 | 12.1 | 11.8 | 14.3 | 14.5 | 14.6 | ≈ 15.4 | 95–96 % |

Each pass needs a fixed NPU time and the NPU runs one at a time, so a
further context only fills the CPU part of another context's pass: the
ceiling is 1 / NPU ms, four contexts hold the NPU 90–96 % busy, and
throughput is flat past four. RSS at eight contexts was 100–165 MB. How the input
reaches the runtime (copy, mem or fd) moved `rknn_run` by under 1 ms.

## What stands between three contexts and a seventh yolox_s camera

The pipeline, 120 s cells (clean = 221 per camera):

| Contexts | CPU governor | yolox_s 640 cameras | Lowest / highest observations | Engine p50 ms | NPU busy | Cairn cores |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | `performance` | 7 | 221 / 221, clean | 229 | 87 % | 0.81 |
| 4 | `schedutil` | 7 | 204 / 207 | 328 | 80 % | 1.29 |
| 4 | `performance` | 7 | 221 / 221, clean | 297 | 86 % | 0.81 |
| 4 | `performance` | 8 | 196 / 197 (14.2 passes/s) | 296 | 88 % | 0.87 |

Scout and boundary samples at three contexts, seven cameras and
`schedutil` read 11.5 passes/s with the NPU 76 % busy, the process on 1.35
cores and the CPU at 1434 MHz mean; at `performance` the CPU read 1.80 GHz. `schedutil` clocks the CPU down
between the short bursts of a pass, which stretches the CPU part of every
pass and so the gap between NPU jobs. A fourth context without the governor
change still misses at seven; with it, three and four both hold seven, and
eight is out of the NPU's reach (14.2 of the 14.5 passes/s plateau).

The governor is a host setting, not something this add-on sets. The
budgets above are at `schedutil`.

## Not measured

- No boundary cell ran with the `performance` governor; its seven cameras
  are 120 s cells and no budget counts them.
- yolox_tiny at 640 was not run at more than one context.
- No boundary cell ran above 2 fps or longer than 300 s.
- Memory under a full add-on. The cells record it for the harness alone: at
  thirteen tiny-416 cameras and three contexts the container's main process
  held ≈ 239 MB RSS and `MemAvailable` fell from ≈ 2.71 to ≈ 2.34 GB, swap
  untouched, the SoC at 36–37 °C; six yolox_s 640 cameras read ≈ 240 MB and
  ≈ 2.42 GB. No recording, main streams or Home Assistant add-on ran beside
  them.
